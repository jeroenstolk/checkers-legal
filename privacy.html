Privacy Policy — 3D Checkers

Last updated: 15 September 2026

3D Checkers is a checkers game for Android. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, who receives it, and what you can do about it.

The app has no user accounts, no login, no chat, and no server of its own. Nothing you do in the game is stored anywhere under our control. We never see, hold, or have access to any data about you as an individual.

Who is responsible

Jeroen Stolk is the data controller for this app. Contact: info@jeroen.dk

What is collected, and by whom

All data collection in this app is performed by Google, through two services built into it. We receive only aggregate, anonymous statistics from them — never information that identifies you.

Service What it collects Why
Google AdMob
(advertising)		 Advertising ID, IP address, device type and operating system, and interactions with ads shown in the app. To show ads, which is how the app is funded, and to measure whether those ads worked.
Google Analytics for Firebase
(usage statistics)		 A randomly generated app-instance identifier, device model, operating system version and approximate country, plus in-game events: when a game starts, when it ends, who won, and whether the players were human or computer. To understand how the game is actually played and which parts need improving.

The game also stores your chosen table surface colour on your own device, using the browser storage built into the app. That preference never leaves your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Legal basis

  • Advertising and analytics: your consent, requested the first time you open the app if you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland.
  • If you decline, the app still works. You will see non-personalised ads, which use far less data.

Changing or withdrawing your consent

You can change your choice at any time from the privacy options in the app's menu. You can also reset your advertising identifier, or opt out of personalised ads entirely, in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

How long data is kept

Analytics event data is retained for 14 months, after which Google deletes it automatically. Advertising data is governed by Google's own retention policies.

Who else receives data

Ads may be supplied by Google's advertising partners, who receive the data described above in order to select and measure ads. The current list is shown in the consent screen inside the app, under “Manage options”, and in Google's list of ad technology providers.

Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

This app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we hold no data about you ourselves, requests about advertising or analytics data must generally be made to Google. Write to info@jeroen.dk and we will help where we can.

If you are in Denmark and believe your data has been mishandled, you may complain to Datatilsynet, the Danish Data Protection Agency.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it.
